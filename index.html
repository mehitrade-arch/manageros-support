Welcome to ManagerOS Support

This page provides general help, troubleshooting information, and a way to contact ManagerOS Support.

Getting Started

Create and Manage Projects

Use ManagerOS to create projects and organize project information, tasks, milestones, deadlines, risks, meetings, and other project-management records.

Project Analysis

ManagerOS includes project-management tools for areas such as risk management, ROAM, SWOT analysis, reporting, planning, and project monitoring.

AI Manager

AI Manager provides AI-assisted management guidance based on your request and relevant project context. AI responses are intended to support your judgment and decision-making and should be reviewed before acting on important recommendations.

Notifications and Reminders

ManagerOS may provide notifications and reminders for supported activities such as project due dates and meetings when these features are enabled.

If notifications are not appearing, check that notification permission is enabled for ManagerOS in your device settings and verify that the relevant reminder is enabled inside the app.

Sharing and Exporting

Supported ManagerOS information can be shared or exported using sharing options available on your device.

Available destinations may depend on the applications and services installed and configured on your device.

Troubleshooting

AI Manager is unavailable

Confirm that your device has an active internet connection. AI-assisted features require network access to process requests.

An AI request does not complete

Check your internet connection and try the request again. Temporary service or network interruptions can prevent a request from completing.

Notifications are not appearing

Check your device notification settings and make sure ManagerOS has permission to send notifications.

The app is not displaying updated information

Return to the relevant screen or reopen ManagerOS. If the issue continues, contact ManagerOS Support with a description of what happened.

When reporting a problem, please describe the feature you were using, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Screenshots can also be helpful. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, medical information, or other highly sensitive information.
